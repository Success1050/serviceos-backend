# V2 Enterprise & Franchise Roadmap (Staged)

This document outlines the upcoming stages for the ServiceOS Enterprise Tier, focusing on multi-level organizations, HQ/Branch hierarchies, and advanced staff management. These stages will commence after the completion of V1.

## Stage 17: Enterprise Hierarchy & Shared Branding
- **Parent-Child Tenants:** Add the ability for an HQ (Parent Tenant) to create Sub-Companies (Child Tenants).
- **Brand Inheritance:** Sub-companies automatically inherit logos, color schemes, and branding from the Parent Company. Sub-companies will have a restricted settings panel, preventing them from overriding global HQ rules.
- **Custom Dashboards:** Sub-company staff get their own localized, distinct dashboards, but the UI is framed within the Parent Company's brand identity.

## Stage 18: Departments & Advanced Staff Management
- **HQ Departments:** HQ can create internal departments (e.g., Finance, Sales, Field Techs) and assign staff to them.
- **Sub-Company Staff Approvals:** Sub-companies can invite their own regional staff, BUT the Parent Company (HQ) must explicitly approve the invite before the account is provisioned.

## Stage 19: HQ Announcements (Bulletin Board) & Internal Ticketing
- **Targeted Announcements:** HQ can publish updates globally to all branches, or target specific branches.
- **Internal Escalation:** Branches can submit internal tickets to HQ (e.g., Marketing Budget Request) triggering both System and Email dual-notifications to Corporate admins.

## Stage 20: Hybrid RBAC (Permissions Engine)
- **Dynamic Roles:** Move away from hardcoded enums to a dynamic `Role` table with granular `Permissions` (e.g., `manage_users`, `view_financials`).
- **Global vs Local Roles:** Parent companies can create global roles that cascade to sub-companies. Sub-companies can create their own localized roles.
- **Direct Permissions:** Support for adding specific override permissions directly to a user, circumventing their role if needed.

# The Final Stretch (Elite SaaS Features)

## Stage 21: Enterprise Analytics Hub
- **HQ "God-View" & Drill-Down:** Corporate sees aggregate network metrics (Total Revenue, Franchise Footprint, System-wide volume). HQ can use a dropdown to drill down into the exact analytics of any specific branch.
- **Sub-Company Local View:** Local branch managers are locked to their own tenant, viewing only their local revenue, job pipelines, and technician performance leaderboards.
- **Internal Ledger:** Invisible P&L tracking allowing HQ to see the internal cost of supplies sent to branches vs. the revenue generated by that branch.

## Stage 22: Advanced Dispatch & Time Tracking
- **Smart Scheduling:** Conflict engine prevents double-booking technicians.
- **Time Tracking:** Techs clock "En Route", "Start Job", and "Finish Job" for accurate payroll, travel-time analytics, and real-time status updates.
- **Geolocation Prep:** Storing GPS coordinates to eventually power a live map of all fleet trucks.

## Stage 23: Job Execution & Virtual Warehouses
- **HQ Hub-and-Spoke Supply Chain:** HQ owns the master catalog and massive warehouses. Sub-companies submit `EquipmentRequests`. HQ approves and the system auto-routes the request to the closest HQ warehouse with stock.
- **Transfer to Virtual Trucks:** Once dispatched, parts move from the HQ Warehouse into the Sub-Company's "Virtual Truck" inventory.
- **Fraud Prevention (Expense Approvals):** If a tech buys parts outside the warehouse (e.g., Home Depot), they must snap a photo of the physical receipt. A manager must approve it before it touches the invoice or reimburses the tech.
- **OTP Job Completion (Uber-Style):** To prevent disputes, the customer receives a 4-digit PIN when the job starts. The technician *must* enter this PIN into their app to close the job, providing undeniable cryptographic proof that the customer authorized the completion.

## Stage 24: Enterprise Payments & Financial Escrow
- **Multi-Gateway Adapter:** Built to support both Stripe (US/Global) and Paystack/Flutterwave (Africa) for seamless cross-border SaaS scaling.
- **Mandatory T&C:** Customers must digitally sign Terms & Conditions when approving a quote, legally authorizing holds and billing schedules.
- **Defense Layer 1 (Auth & Capture):** For 1-7 day jobs, the system automatically places a "Hold" on the customer's card 24 hours prior to arrival. When the tech enters the OTP (Stage 23), the backend instantly captures the held funds.
- **Milestone Billing (Massive Projects):** For massive projects, the Estimator overrides the billing type on the Quote to "Milestone" (e.g., 30% start, 30% mid, 40% completion). 

## Stage 25: The Customer Portal Experience
- **Live Tracking:** Customers log in to see appointment times, technician bios/photos, and real-time status ("Tech is on the way").
- **Self-Serve History:** Access to all past invoices, digital records, and a direct pipeline to request new service calls straight to the branch's queue.

## Stage 26: Customer Support Hub (Completed - Backend)
- **Ticketing & Disputes:** A centralized hub where customers can log warranty requests, dispute charges, or chat with the Sub-Company's front desk directly through the portal.
- **Warranty Claims Engine:** Customers can select registered assets with auto-verification of warranty expiration. Approving a claim automatically provisions a $0 warranty rework job linked to the asset and customer.
- **Automated Charge Dispute Protection:** Filing a billing dispute instantly freezes the invoice (`DISPUTED`), halting automated payment collection and dunning. Resolving a dispute provides audited actions: upholding charges with documented justification, issuing partial credit notes, or executing gateway refunds (Stripe / Paystack) and releasing escrow holds.
- **2-Way Live Front Desk Chat & Internal Notes:** Bi-directional messaging thread between customer portal and branch support desk with read receipts and private staff-only internal notes (`isInternalNote: true`) guarded from customer visibility.
- **Dual-Channel Real-Time Notifications:** Instant event push to both the tenant front desk queue and customer portal via WebSockets (`NotificationGateway`) and in-app notifications.
- **Executive SLA & Dispute Metrics:** Backoffice dashboard tracking open dispute counts, pending warranty claims, urgent tickets, and average resolution times.

## Stage 27: Blue-Collar HR & SMS Onboarding (Completed - Backend)
- **"Over-the-Desk" Setup:** Branch managers can onboard field workers using only a phone number, trade specialty, and custom commission rate (zero email dependency, with deterministic shadow identities for seamless database compatibility).
- **Digital HR Dossier & Document Management:** Multi-document repository (`HrDocument`) attaching national IDs, live over-the-desk face photos, trade certifications (HVAC, Electrical, Plumbing), and PDF resumes directly to the technician's permanent file.
- **HQ Proxy Verification Engine:** Corporate HQ God-View dashboard across all franchisee branches to audit manager endorsements and physical ID evidence. HQ approves or rejects hires with feedback, maintaining corporate brand governance and dispatch security.
- **Passwordless 4-Digit SMS PIN Login:** Field technicians authenticate into their mobile web app / PWA using a fast, 4-digit numeric PIN sent via SMS (rate-limited, cryptographic generation, replay protection, and zero passwords to remember or reset).
- **The "Big Button" Legal Compensation Acknowledgment:** Upon first mobile login, technicians review a clear commission disclosure and tap a massive "I AGREE" button, generating an immutable `AuditLog` entry (timestamp, IP address, user-agent, rate snapshot) providing undeniable legal payroll protection against labor disputes.
- **Offline & Feature-Phone ("Button Phone") Dispatch Pipeline:** Recognizing that many blue-collar field technicians in emerging markets operate basic feature phones (Nokia/itel/Tecno torchlight phones) without smartphones, mobile data, or web browsers:
  - When jobs are scheduled, assigned, or rescheduled, the system automatically triggers structured 160-character plain text SMS briefs directly to the technician's mobile number (`+234...`).
  - Contains all critical field parameters: Job Title, Client Name, Premise Address, Time Slot, and Customer Phone contact.
  - Accompanied by optional email notifications for technicians with email-enabled devices.
  - Provides dispatchers with a manual "Resend SMS Work Order" button in the Job Dossier to re-dispatch anytime.

## Stage 28: Fintech Wallets & Multi-Tenant Payroll (Completed - Backend)
- **The 3-Tier Wallet System:** 
  1. *HQ Wallet (`WalletType.HQ`):* Accumulates network franchise royalties automatically via Split-Payments (`ROYALTY_SPLIT`).
  2. *Branch Wallet (`WalletType.BRANCH`):* Accumulates local job revenues (`JOB_SETTLEMENT`), automatically debited for HQ royalties and staff commissions, retaining net operating profits.
  3. *Staff Wallet (`WalletType.STAFF`):* Field technicians visually see their earned commission balances grow in real-time (`COMMISSION_EARNED`) upon verified OTP job completion.
- **Dynamic Compensation & Smart Splits:** Managers set custom commission rates per technician (`customCommissionRate`, e.g. 15%) or fall back to signed compensation acknowledgments (`agreedCommissionSnapshot`). The system automatically validates allocation boundaries and ensures branch net profit integrity.
- **Paystack Transfers & Banking Rails Integration:** Technicians verify 10-digit NUBAN bank accounts using Paystack Bank Resolution (`/bank/resolve`), automatically generating Paystack Transfer Recipient tokens (`RCP_...`) for frictionless withdrawals.
- **Idempotent Double-Entry Ledger Engine:** Every financial movement is recorded as an immutable `WalletTransaction` with cryptographically unique references, `balanceBefore`, and `balanceAfter`, guaranteeing complete auditability.
- **Automated Payout & Approval Workflow:** Technicians submit withdrawal requests (`PayoutRequest`), atomically reserving funds (`lockedBalance`). Branch managers review and approve payouts, triggering automated Paystack transfers (`/transfer`) with webhook listeners (`transfer.success`, `transfer.failed`, `transfer.reversed`) and audit logs.

## Stage 29: SuperAdmin Control Plane, Subscription Tiers & Module Feature-Gating (Completed - Backend)
- **Platform Owner Root Authority:** The `SUPERADMIN` (`PLATFORM_ADMIN`) is the master owner of the ServiceOS SaaS platform, operating above individual tenants with global governance, platform telemetry, and billing control.
- **Full Hierarchy Visibility (Parent Enterprises & Nested Sub-Companies):**
  - SuperAdmin can view, search, and inspect the entire multi-tenant tree:
    - Root Platform → Parent Tenants (Corporate Franchisors, e.g. Sunshine Solar Global) → Child Tenants / Sub-Companies (e.g. Lagos Island Branch, Abuja Branch, Port Harcourt Branch).
  - Inspect aggregate operational volume, active technician headcounts, and monthly revenue generated across each parent enterprise and all its child sub-companies.
- **Audited 1-Click Tenant Impersonation:**
  - SuperAdmin can generate short-lived, cryptographically audited impersonation tokens to log into any parent tenant or child sub-company context directly to investigate issues, debug dispatch queues, or provide white-glove technical assistance.
  - All impersonation actions are recorded with `AuditLog` entries specifying the SuperAdmin's real ID, timestamp, and target tenant.
- **Subscription Tier Modeling & Cascading Rules:**
  - Introduce `SubscriptionPlan` model with predefined plans (`STARTER`, `GROWTH`, `ENTERPRISE`, plus custom enterprise contracts).
  - Tiers can be assigned to a Parent Tenant and automatically cascade down to all its sub-companies, with optional per-branch feature overrides.
- **Dynamic Module Restriction & Feature Gating:**
  - Granular restriction of business modules based on active subscription tier:
    1. `MODULE_CRM` (Customer CRM & CSV/Excel Import)
    2. `MODULE_QUOTES` (Quote Builder & PDF Engine)
    3. `MODULE_INVOICES` (Invoicing & Multi-Gateway Checkout)
    4. `MODULE_DISPATCH` (Smart Scheduling Calendar & GPS Conflict Engine)
    5. `MODULE_WAREHOUSE` (Virtual Trucks & Warehouse Inventory)
    6. `MODULE_CUSTOMER_PORTAL` (White-Labeled Customer Portal `/s/:slug`)
    7. `MODULE_SUPPORT_DESK` (Ticketing, Warranty Claims & Charge Disputes)
    8. `MODULE_BLUE_COLLAR_HR` (Phone-Only Onboarding & SMS PIN Auth)
    9. `MODULE_FINTECH_WALLETS` (3-Tier Wallets, Paystack Split Settlements & Payroll)
    10. `MODULE_ENTERPRISE_HQ` (Franchise Hierarchy, God-View & Royalties)
- **Declarative Guard (`@RequireModule('MODULE_KEY')`):**
  - NestJS guard attached to controllers/endpoints verifying that the caller's tenant has the required module enabled in their subscription tier or tenant override.
  - If a module is restricted, throws HTTP 402 `PaymentRequiredException` (`"Module restricted by active subscription tier. Contact your administrator or upgrade your plan."`).
- **SuperAdmin Control Plane API:**
  - Dedicated SuperAdmin endpoints to toggle module gates per tenant, adjust seat/technician quotas, suspend an entire franchise network or single rogue branch, and view network-wide MRR telemetry.
